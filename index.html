La nostra storia

A family kitchen on Highwood Hill

The Rising Sun has welcomed guests on Highwood Hill since the sixteenth century. Today it is run by the Delnevo brothers, who cook the food their Italian family always has: honest, generous and made to be shared, with a few flavours picked up from further east.

Many of our guests have been coming for years, for a quiet lunch, a long Sunday or a birthday around the big table. However often you visit, you will be looked after like one of the family.

— The Delnevo family

Tutti a tavola

A table for everyone

  • Families and little ones

    High chairs, smaller portions and a kitchen happy to keep it simple for fussy eaters.

  • Dogs welcome too

    Muddy paws from the Mill Hill walks are fine by us. There's always a bowl of water by the door.

  • A garden for summer

    Benches under the wisteria, good music and long lunches that turn into early dinners.

  • Birthdays and get-togethers

    Tell us what you're celebrating when you book and we'll do our best to give you the right room.
