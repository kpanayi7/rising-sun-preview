Our family

Two brothers, one old pub, and a lot of regulars who feel like cousins

The Rising Sun has been pouring pints on Highwood Hill since the 1500s. Today it's looked after by the Delnevo brothers, who brought their Italian family's cooking with them and added a little of the East along the way.

Plenty of our guests have been coming for years. They know which table catches the evening sun and exactly how they like their pizza. Come once and we'll remember you next time.

With love, the Delnevo brothers

Everyone's welcome

Room at the table for all of you

  • Families and little ones

    High chairs, smaller portions and a kitchen happy to keep it simple for fussy eaters.

  • Dogs welcome too

    Muddy paws from the Mill Hill walks are fine by us. There's always a bowl of water by the door.

  • A garden for summer

    Benches under the wisteria, good music and long lunches that turn into early dinners.

  • Birthdays and get-togethers

    Tell us what you're celebrating when you book and we'll do our best to give you the right room.
